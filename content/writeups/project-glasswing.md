The cybersecurity world may have just entered a completely new era.

Anthropic recently announced **Project Glasswing**, a major cybersecurity initiative involving some of the biggest technology companies in the world, including Amazon Web Services, Apple, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Palo Alto Networks, and JPMorganChase.

### The goal of the project is simple but extremely important:

Use advanced AI systems to protect critical software and digital infrastructure before cybercriminals can attack them.

At the center of this initiative is a powerful unreleased AI model called **Claude Mythos Preview**.

According to Anthropic, this AI model has already discovered thousands of serious security vulnerabilities across major operating systems, browsers, and widely used software tools. Some of these vulnerabilities had remained hidden for decades, even after years of testing by security experts and automated security tools.

One example was a **27-year-old vulnerability in OpenBSD**, a system known for being highly secure.

Another was a **16-year-old vulnerability in FFmpeg**, a software library used by millions of applications worldwide.

The AI was even able to combine multiple vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel to gain complete control of systems automatically.

This is a major warning sign for the cybersecurity industry.

For a long time, finding and exploiting software vulnerabilities required highly skilled hackers or security researchers. But AI is now changing that reality very quickly.

We are entering a time where AI can:

- Find vulnerabilities faster
- Build exploits faster
- Automate cyberattacks
- Scale hacking operations
- Reduce the technical skills needed for offensive attacks

In short, AI is making cyber capabilities more powerful and more accessible.

But there is also good news.

The same AI technology that could help attackers can also help defenders protect systems faster and more effectively.

That is the main idea behind Project Glasswing.

Instead of waiting for attackers to use advanced AI first, major technology companies are working together to use AI defensively:

- Finding zero-day vulnerabilities early
- Securing open-source software
- Improving patch management
- Strengthening software security
- Protecting critical infrastructure

Anthropic is reportedly committing up to **$100 million in AI usage credits** and additional funding for open-source security organizations to support this effort.

This announcement shows that cybersecurity is changing rapidly.

Traditional security methods alone may no longer be enough in the AI era.

The future of cybersecurity may become:

**AI defending systems against AI-powered attacks.**

That means organizations, governments, and security professionals must start preparing now.

### Critical sectors like:

- Banking
- Healthcare
- Energy
- Transportation
- Government systems
- Telecommunications

All depend heavily on software infrastructure that could become targets for AI-assisted cyberattacks.

Project Glasswing is not just a technology project.

It is an early attempt to prepare the world for a future where AI becomes one of the most powerful tools in both cyber defense and cyber offense.

The cybersecurity race is evolving fast.

And the organizations that adapt early may have the best chance of staying secure in the years ahead.
